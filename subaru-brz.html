03 / AUTOMOTIVE / HANDS-ON RESTORATION

Restore. Refine. Drive.

A hands-on restoration combining body repair, component replacement, reassembly, and manual-transmission troubleshooting.

MY ROLE
Restoration team member · repair, parts sourcing & reassembly
WHEN
2024 — 2025
STATUS
Restoration work

The challenge

Assess an auction-sourced car, plan repairs, source parts, and return the vehicle to a presentable, usable condition while tracking costs.

My contribution

Working with a small team and experienced mechanic support, I helped with body repair, replacement components, cleaning, paint preparation, and reassembly. I also participated in troubleshooting manual-transmission shifting and suspected synchronizer-related issues.

Design decisions

The work connected diagnosis to practical repair planning: identify the affected components, find suitable replacements, and check fit as the car went back together. Parts availability and cost informed the repair sequence.

Evidence & current outcome

The original project photographs show the damaged front, work in the engine bay, rear body preparation, and the car following exterior restoration. They document the work visually without implying a measured performance or reliability result.

What comes next

This project strengthened my approach to disassembly, mechanical troubleshooting, and organizing repairs around real parts and constraints.
