04 / AUTOMOTIVE / DIAGNOSTICS & REPAIR

A non-runner. A second chance.

Helping turn a $500 non-running auction car into a drivable vehicle through diagnosis, repair, and exterior restoration.

MY ROLE
Restoration team member · diagnosis, repair planning & reassembly
WHEN
2024 — 2025
STATUS
Returned to drivable condition

The challenge

The team acquired a non-running 2013 Dodge Dart through a dealership-auction connection. Transmission and shift-linkage issues had to be investigated alongside exterior damage.

My contribution

I helped assess repair needs, source parts, track costs, troubleshoot transmission and shifting issues, and carry out component repair or replacement and exterior restoration with the team.

Design decisions

The work centered on diagnosing the shifting problem before selecting repairs. Preparing the vehicle for resale also required attention to body condition, reassembly, and presentation.

Evidence & current outcome

The team returned the car to drivable condition. The original photos document its auction condition, exterior damage, and appearance after the work. The $500 figure is the purchase price, not the complete restoration cost or profit.

What comes next

The project built practical experience in cost-aware repair planning, teamwork, and following a mechanical problem from initial symptoms through reassembly.

More work. More ways to solve a problem.
